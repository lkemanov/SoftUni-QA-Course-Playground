Sections 3 to 8 apply to all cases. The data of our own staff is processed under separate internal rules, which they receive when they join.

SECTION B. Employees receiving occupational health services

B.1. Where we get the data

We do not collect this data through the website. We receive it from:

  • your employer, when concluding and performing the occupational health service contract;
  • you, when you visit our service on your own initiative or during training and consultations;
  • the medical centres and doctors who carried out your pre-employment and periodic medical examinations;
  • your employer’s previous occupational health service, when your health record is transferred to us officially.

B.2. What data we process

  • Identification data: first name, middle name, surname, personal identification number (EGN), permanent address, place of birth.
  • Employment data: employer, position (occupation), workplace, working conditions and risk factors, education, length of service and career history (previous employers, positions and duration).
  • Data about your general practitioner: name, address and phone number.
  • Health data: results of pre-employment and periodic medical examinations and tests, medical and family history, chronic and past illnesses, periods of temporary incapacity for work from copies of sick notes, registered occupational diseases, workplace accidents, job adaptation, permanent reduction in working capacity, expert decisions of the medical expert commissions (TELK and NELK), and decisions of the National Social Security Institute (NOI) recognising an accident as work-related.
  • Data about visits to our service: date, reason and action taken.

B.3. Purposes and legal bases

PurposeLegal basis
Occupational health services under the Bulgarian Health and Safety at Work Act and Ordinance No. 3 of 25.01.2008: keeping health records, monitoring health, fitness-for-work decisions, assessing health risksLegal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) and processing for the purposes of occupational medicine and the assessment of working capacity by persons bound by professional secrecy (Art. 9(2)(h) and 9(3))
Analysing employees’ health and proposing improvements to working conditionsThe same legal bases. The employer receives the analysis only in aggregated form, without data that could identify a specific person.
Providing data to competent authorities and courtsLegal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) and Art. 9(2)(f) or (h), as applicable

We do not ask for your consent to this processing, because it is required by law. We do not use this data for marketing or any other purpose.

B.4. Who receives the data

  • Your employer receives only what the law allows: a decision on whether you are fit to do the job, recommendations on working conditions and aggregated analyses. Your employer does not receive diagnoses or your medical information.
  • Another occupational health service, when your employer changes provider, receives your health record through official channels.
  • Public authorities and courts, when they request it in writing on a legal basis, for example the Labour Inspectorate, the Regional Health Inspectorate, NOI, TELK and NELK, courts, prosecutors and investigating authorities, and the Commission for Personal Data Protection.

We do not transfer data from health records outside the European Union.

B.5. Retention period

Employees’ health records are kept for 50 years. When the contract with the employer ends, the record is transferred to the new occupational health service through official channels or kept by us until the period ends. The data is then securely destroyed.

B.6. How we protect the data

  • Health records are kept in specialised software for occupational health services and on paper.
  • The computer with the database is not connected to a network, and access requires a personal password that is changed every quarter.
  • Paper records are kept in a locked cabinet in a locked room in our office.
  • Records are not taken out of the office, and copying the database to portable media is prohibited.
  • Only the doctors and staff who work directly with the records have access. All are bound by medical confidentiality or a signed confidentiality declaration and receive data protection training.

B.7. Your rights regarding health data

You have the rights described in Section 5, with the following specifics:

  • Access: you can obtain information about and a copy of the data in your health record.
  • Correction: you can ask us to correct inaccurate data. Medical conclusions and test results are not changed, but we can add your note or a new document.
  • Erasure and objection: while the legal retention period runs, we cannot delete the data or stop processing it, because the law requires us to keep it.

When we act as a processor under a contract with your employer, we will inform your employer of your request within 5 working days and help it reply to you.

3. Transfers outside the European Economic Area

Data is transferred outside the EEA only to run the website (Section A.5), where a provider is in the USA or another country outside the EEA. We do so only with one of the following safeguards:

  • an adequacy decision of the European Commission (Art. 45), including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the recipient is certified under it;
  • standard contractual clauses adopted by the European Commission (Art. 46(2)(c)).

On request we will tell you which safeguard applies to each recipient and give you a copy of it. Health data is not transferred outside the European Union.

4. Automated decisions

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing and we do not carry out profiling (Art. 22). Fitness-for-work decisions are made by a doctor.

5. Your rights

You have the right to request:

  • access to your data and a copy of it (Art. 15);
  • correction of inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • erasure (Art. 17);
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • portability of the data you gave us (Art. 20).

Right to object (Art. 21). You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest. We will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the data is needed for a legal claim.

Some rights are limited where the law requires us to keep the data (see Section B.7).

How to exercise your rights. Write to office@life-04.com or by post to the address in Section 2. We reply free of charge within one month. For complex or numerous requests this can be extended by up to two months, and we will tell you if so (Art. 12(3)). We will ask you to confirm your identity before disclosing data, especially health data, so that we do not disclose it to someone else.

Complaints. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection: 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., 1592 Sofia, www.cpdp.bg. You can also seek a remedy in court (Art. 79).

6. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures suited to the type of data. The website runs only over a secure connection (HTTPS). Access to email and databases is limited to the staff who need it and is password-protected. The measures for health records are described in Section B.6. In the event of a personal data breach we act under Art. 33 and 34 of the Regulation and promptly inform the employer when we act as a processor on its behalf.
