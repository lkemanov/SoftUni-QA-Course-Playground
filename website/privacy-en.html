3. What data we process

Data you enter in the form:

  • company, phone and email (required);
  • name, number of employees and the text of your message (optional).

Technical data: IP address, browser and device type, date and time of the visit. They are logged by the hosting provider and by the “I am human” check (hCaptcha).

Data in browser storage: the times of enquiries sent in the last hour. They stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Health data: we do not ask for it and it is not needed for an enquiry. Please do not include information about your health or anyone else’s in your message. If we receive such information anyway, we will not use it and will delete it without undue delay.

10. Your rights

You have the right to request:

  • access to your data and a copy of it (Art. 15);
  • correction of inaccurate data (Art. 16);
  • erasure (Art. 17);
  • restriction of processing (Art. 18);
  • portability of the data you gave us (Art. 20).

Right to object (Art. 21). You can object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interest. We will stop unless we have compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the data is needed for a legal claim.

We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing and we do not carry out profiling (Art. 22).

How to exercise your rights. Write to office@life-04.com or to our Data Protection Officer. We reply free of charge within one month. For complex or numerous requests this can be extended by up to two months, and we will tell you if so (Art. 12(3)). We may ask you to confirm your identity so that we do not disclose data to someone else.

Complaints. If you believe your rights have been violated, you can complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection: 2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., 1592 Sofia, www.cpdp.bg. You can also seek a remedy in court (Art. 79).

11. Security

The website runs only over a secure connection (HTTPS). Access to the enquiry mailbox is limited to the staff who answer enquiries and is password-protected. In the event of a personal data breach we act under Art. 33 and 34 of the Regulation.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, we publish the new version on this page with a new effective date. For significant changes we will inform the people we are in active contact with.
